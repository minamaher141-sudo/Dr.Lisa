My Story

From Passion to Purpose

My journey into nutrition coaching started with a personal struggle. Growing up, I watched family members struggle with their weight, trying every fad diet and extreme program with no lasting results. I knew there had to be a better way.

After earning my degree in Clinical Nutrition and Dietetics, I dedicated my career to developing evidence-based, personalized approaches to weight management. I've spent over 8 years studying how food affects our bodies, our energy, and our mental health.

Today, I've helped hundreds of clients achieve their health goals without sacrificing the foods they love or following impossible restrictions. My approach is simple: understand your body, respect your lifestyle, and create sustainable habits that last a lifetime.

"I believe everyone deserves to feel confident, energetic, and healthy in their own body - without suffering through extreme diets."

My Mission

To empower people to take control of their health through personalized, science-based nutrition coaching that respects their individuality and lifestyle.

My Vision

A world where healthy eating is accessible, enjoyable, and sustainable for everyone - not just a privilege for the few who can afford extreme programs.

My Values

Compassion, science, sustainability, and authenticity. I believe in honest guidance, realistic expectations, and celebrating every small victory along the way.

My Approach

Nutrition Philosophy

01

No One-Size-Fits-All

Your body is unique. Your nutrition plan should be too. I create completely personalized strategies based on your metabolism, preferences, and goals.

02

Food is Not the Enemy

I don't believe in cutting out entire food groups or making you fear carbs. Good nutrition is about balance, not restriction.

03

Sustainability Over Speed

Quick fixes lead to quick rebounds. I focus on building habits that will keep you healthy for decades, not just weeks.

04

Science Meets Compassion

Every recommendation I make is evidence-based, but I deliver it with empathy, understanding, and genuine care for your wellbeing.
